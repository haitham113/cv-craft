# Approved CV Craft policy summary (85 decisions)

Universal audience; existing or scratch; job optional; supplied/authorized sources; hybrid fact verification; deep employer research and sequential candidate interview; relevance-first writing; adaptive length and language. PDF + DOCX outputs, interactive review, comprehensive conversation QA, privacy-first temporary storage. Broad CVs may omit target role and job-fit score. Candidate only: conversation participant can approve without identity verification.

Research: employer official sources first; third-party context only. Candidate profiles limited to authorized sources. Approval before employer-fit, fit weights, drafting, design/title/gap/link choices, release, external services and disclosure. All changes to approved content require new approval.

Evidence: claim-level citations; documented, candidate-confirmed, or candidate-approved qualified approximate metrics. Confidential details need case-by-case checks. Skills should be evidenced; AI work highlighted when role-relevant. Always include truthful headline and professional summary; group skills; adapt education/project sections.

Fit: matrix + qualitative analysis + transparent weighted numerical estimate, score verified requirements only, report unverified total separately and flag missing mandatory requirements. No fictional ATS or interview probability.

Engineering: modular SKILL.md, scripts and references; JSON Schema canonical structure with schema versions; independent PDF/DOCX; Node-first (24 preferred, 22 tested) and npm lockfile; approved-dependency-only auto-install, portable local/remote; system fonts with fallbacks; enhanced accessibility. Approvals stored in managed workspace; persistent retention only by consent. Explicit closure, no inactivity deletion. Free optional ATS services with explicit full-CV consent; local validation fallback. Strong threat reporting; comprehensive tests and owner approval before release.
